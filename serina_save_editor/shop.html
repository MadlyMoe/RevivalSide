Welcome to Serina Crew's Shop!

Hello boss! This tool allows you to safely manage your game account in real-time.

  • Shop (Add Item): Send units, items, and gears directly to your in-game Mailbox. Mails expire in 3 minutes.
  • Gear Tuning: Clicking "Queue" on an Equipment allows you to customize its Set and Substats before sending! You can also Edit it in the pending queue.
  • Refund (Remove): Delete unwanted items from your inventory. Warning: Do not delete Units/Ships that are currently in a squad/deck!
  • Queue System: Click "Queue" to add multiple actions, edit their quantities, then click Accept Changes.

* Pro Tip: After adding items, just open the Mailbox in-game. No need to restart the game!
